# Mechanics

## Player

- Moves with WASD or arrow keys.
- Has HP.
- Attacks automatically.

## Enemy

- Chases the player.
- Has HP.
- Deals damage on contact.
- Drops EXP when defeated.

## Projectile

- Fired automatically.
- Targets the nearest enemy.
- Deals damage.
- Disappears on hit.

## Level Up

Temporary upgrade examples:

- Move speed +10%
- Attack damage +10%
- Attack cooldown -10%
- Max HP +20
- Projectile count +1

## Permanent Upgrade

Permanent upgrade examples:

- Starting HP +10
- Starting damage +5%
- Resource gain +5%
